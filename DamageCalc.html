⚠️ This tool uses standard Pokémon battle formulas at Level 50, fixed IV 31, with a maximum of 252 EVs per stat and 510 total EVs. Nature, Ability, item, weather, terrain, critical hits, Helping Hand, STAB, type matchups, and 39 random damage rolls are applied. HP-based abilities only activate when the low-HP condition is enabled. This calculator does not include shields, burn, prior-turn stat boosts, or other situational effects.

Damage algorithm
  1. Compute Level 50 stats from base stats, IV 31, EVs, and Nature: HP = floor((2 × Base + IV + floor(EV/4)) × 50/100) + 60; other stats = floor((floor((2 × Base + IV + floor(EV/4)) × 50/100) + 5) × Nature).
  2. Apply stage changes, stat-altering abilities, stat items, and HP/weather conditions.
  3. Determine move type, base power after abilities, STAB, and type effectiveness.
  4. Apply weather, terrain, damage items, defensive abilities, critical hits, and Helping Hand in modifier order.
  5. Roll 39 random values from 217 to 255; the result is the real min-max damage range.
